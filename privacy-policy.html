1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Protection Téléphone ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Ahmed Becetti, handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and international data protection standards.

2. Data Collection & Processing

We believe in a privacy-first approach. The application functions primarily through on-device, local processing without transmitting your personal photos, files, or sensitive information to any advertising networks.

  • Antivirus Scanning: Scans installed package identifiers and application hashes on your device to cross-reference against virus and malware definition databases.
  • Junk Cleaner: Inspects local temporary application cache and leftover directories solely to free up disk storage space upon your explicit command.
  • RAM & Performance Booster: Reads system memory usage and hardware metrics (temperature, RAM load) to recommend optimization steps.
  • Privacy Auditor: Examines which installed applications hold sensitive device permissions (Camera, Microphone, Location) to help you manage your privacy.
  • AI Security Advisor: Enables you to ask security questions analyzed using Google Gemini AI models.

3. Device Permissions

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Essential to scan installed applications for malicious trojans, viruses, and security vulnerabilities.
  • INTERNET: Required to download cloud virus definition updates, conduct server latency tests, and communicate with the Gemini AI Security Advisor.

4. Third-Party Services

The application interacts with trusted third-party providers:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Gemini AI API

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All security audit logs and cleaner history are stored exclusively in your device's local database. You can permanently wipe all logs at any time via Settings > Clear History.
